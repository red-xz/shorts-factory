# Petrov, 1983 — example script

A worked example for the whole pipeline: script → scenes → build.
Stanislav Petrov, September 26, 1983 — the man who decided whether
the world ended, and decided it didn't.

S1 test: named person (Petrov) ✓, extreme stakes (nuclear war) ✓,
payoff held (did he report it?) ✓, US resonance (Cold War, the war
every American over 60 grew up expecting) ✓.

The narration below is the real script. The example build uses
placeholder tone audio instead of real TTS (see build.sh) so it runs
anywhere — replace `s1.mp3`–`s6.mp3` with real narration and
re-measure the durations before building for real.

## S1 — Hook + promise (8.0s)

Clip: warning screens, red alarm light, a man alone at a console.
Narration: "On September 26, 1983, one man decided whether the world
ended. Five missiles were coming — or so the machines said. Stay with
me, because what he did next saved everyone watching this."
Captions: "ONE MAN. ONE DECISION." (gold) / "FIVE MISSILES WERE COMING"

## S2 — Place and time (9.0s)

Clip: a concrete bunker, rows of consoles, night shift.
Narration: "It was just past midnight at Serpukhov-15, a secret bunker
south of Moscow. Lieutenant Colonel Stanislav Petrov was on duty,
watching the satellites that watched America."
Captions: "SERPUKHOV-15, NEAR MOSCOW" (gold) / "THE ALARM SAID: LAUNCH DETECTED"

## S3 — The mechanism (10.0s)

Clip: satellite view, missile trails arcing, radar screens empty.
Narration: "The system said five Minuteman missiles were in the air.
But the ground radar showed nothing. And Petrov knew something the
machine didn't: a real first strike wouldn't be five missiles."
Captions: "FIVE MINUTEMAN MISSILES" / "GROUND RADAR SHOWED NOTHING" /
"A REAL ATTACK WOULDN'T BE FIVE" (gold)

## S4 — The turn (9.0s)

Clip: Petrov at the console, the phone to Moscow, the clock.
Narration: "Protocol said report it up the chain — and a report meant
retaliation. He had minutes. The system was screaming launch, and
every rule he had said to believe it."
Captions: "PROTOCOL SAID: REPORT IT" / "REPORTING MEANT RETALIATION" (gold) /
"HE HAD MINUTES"

## S5 — The climax (9.0s)

Clip: the phone going down, the screens going quiet, dawn.
Narration: "He called it a false alarm. He was right — sunlight
reflecting off high-altitude clouds had fooled the satellites. The
missiles were never there."
Captions: "HE CALLED IT A FALSE ALARM" (gold) / "SUNLIGHT ON CLOUDS" /
"HE WAS RIGHT"

## S6 — Payoff + question + CTA (11.0s)

Clip: an old man, medals in a drawer, a quiet street.
Narration: "He got no reward and no punishment — the world never knew
his name for years. One man trusted his judgment over the machine, and
everyone alive today owes him. Would you have trusted the machine?
Like, comment, and subscribe for more history they never taught you."
Captions: "NO REWARD. NO PUNISHMENT." / "THE WORLD NEVER KNEW" (gold) /
"WOULD YOU HAVE TRUSTED THE MACHINE?" / "LIKE - COMMENT - SUBSCRIBE" (gold)
